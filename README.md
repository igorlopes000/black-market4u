## 📋 CABEÇALHO DO PROMPT (Obrigatório)

PROJECT: Black Friday 2026 Market4u — Deck comercial de pacotes de mídia para parceiros da indústria

ROLE: Senior Frontend Engineer, Presentation Designer & UI/UX Designer specialized in premium B2B retail media decks

OUTPUT: Web-based presentation deck, 7 slides, 16:9, Portuguese (pt-BR), optimized for 1920x1080 projector and desktop presentation mode

## 🎯 CONTEXT & GOAL

Build a premium commercial presentation deck for the Market4u commercial team to sell Black Friday media packages to industry partners.

Target audience: Commercial management and trade teams from suppliers, manufacturers and industry partners.

The presentation must position Market4u's Black Friday as the major retail moment of ultra-convenience. The campaign narrative is a strategic game board: the available packages are limited pieces, and each level offers greater media priority across the shopper journey.

The deck must be visually impactful, concise and commercial. Each slide must contain only:

- One short title;

- One single-line body;

- A discreet footer, when declared in the slide structure.

Do not add paragraphs, extra bullets, explanatory subtitles or undeclared commercial claims.

Vibe: Dark premium, cinematic, strategic, retail media, ultra-convenience, elegant, high-contrast, minimal, game-board abstraction.

## 🎨 DESIGN SYSTEM (Referenciar documentos canônicos)

DESIGN SYSTEM (FROM `Direcao-de-Arte-Black-Friday-2026-Market4u-v1.md` & `Linguagem-Canonica-Black-Friday-2026-Market4u-v1.md`)

Primary Color: Market4u Green — `#2DBE6C`

Secondary Color: Game Gold — `#D4A017`

Supporting Color: Silver — `#A8B0B8`

Backgrounds: Every slide must use a full-bleed AI-generated background image. Backgrounds must be dark, premium and cinematic, built predominantly on Black Friday Black `#0B0B0B`. Apply a dark overlay to ensure accessibility and contrast.

Typography:  

Headers: Montserrat ExtraBold / Bold, uppercase, white `#FFFFFF`, light tracking  

Body: Inter Medium, off-white `#F5F5F5`  

Footers: Inter Regular, white at 60% opacity  

Numbers and investment values: Montserrat SemiBold or Inter SemiBold

Layout:  

16:9 aspect ratio  

Minimum 1920x1080  

Safe margins between 6% and 8%  

Narrative slides: title and body aligned left  

Cover and closing slides: centered composition  

Architecture and investment slides: title at top and central visual content  

Use title sizing around `clamp(48px, 7vw, 96px)`  

Use body sizing around `clamp(20px, 2.6vw, 34px)`

Style:  

Dark premium retail game board  

Black, green and gold cinematic lighting  

Minimal tech UI  

High-end product lighting  

Subtle grid and strategic-path motifs  

No generic Black Friday retail-poster aesthetic

### Global image rules

Generate all background images through AI. Do not use stock photography or external image banks.

All generated images must:

- Be full bleed;

- Contain no text;

- Contain no letters, numbers, logos, watermarks or signatures;

- Preserve intentional negative space for overlay text;

- Use a premium, minimal composition;

- Avoid busy backgrounds and visual clutter;

- Avoid recognizable faces;

- Avoid military imagery, weapons, camouflage or war references;

- Avoid red-dominant promotional aesthetics;

- Avoid generic low-quality stock-photo appearance.

Global negative prompt:

`text, letters, words, numbers, logos, watermark, signature, cluttered, busy background, low contrast, red dominance, military imagery, weapons, camouflage, war, distorted hands, recognizable faces, stock photo look, banding, noisy gradients`

### Global overlay rules

Use this standard overlay when needed:

`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(11,11,11,0.72), rgba(11,11,11,0.35))`

For centered slides, use a subtle radial black overlay with 45% to 65% opacity.

Never place text directly over a high-detail or high-light area.

## ⚙ EXECUTION RULES (Regras de implementação)

Vibe Coding:

- Build a polished, presentation-first interface rather than a conventional scrolling website.

- Use one viewport-height slide at a time.

- Use Framer Motion for subtle fade transitions and restrained element entrance animations.

- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

- Keep animation secondary to legibility.

- Do not use loud, bouncy or game-like UI effects.

Content:

- Use the exact Portuguese content declared in the presentation structure.

- Do not paraphrase, summarize, translate or expand the slide copy.

- Do not add slides.

- Do not add charts, KPIs, benefits, legal notes or commercial promises not declared in the structure.

- Do not add extra text to the architecture diagram beyond its six approved labels.

- Do not add package descriptions to the investment cards beyond name, value and availability.

Images:

- Generate every background image via AI using the respective image-generation prompt.

- Do not render text, logos, numbers, labels or watermarks inside generated images.

- Add all deck text as HTML/UI overlay layers.

- Use dark overlays to ensure WCAG AA minimum contrast.

- Preserve all required negative spaces for title and body overlays.

- Maintain black, green, gold and silver visual consistency across all slides.

Responsiveness:

- Optimize primarily for 1920x1080 presentation and projector mode.

- Maintain the 16:9 composition on desktop.

- On smaller screens, scale typography and cards proportionally without clipping.

- Preserve readable labels and non-overlapping architecture nodes.

- Avoid horizontal overflow.

Accessibility:

- Ensure minimum WCAG AA contrast for all visible text.

- Provide keyboard navigation.

- Give generated background images meaningful empty alt text if decorative, or descriptive alt text if exposed semantically.

- Do not rely only on color to distinguish package hierarchy; maintain positional and typographic differentiation.
